# Artwork and animation assets

The Kurumi illustrations and transparent animation frames in this repository are generated fan art based on the user-approved chibi reference. Tokisaki Kurumi and *Date A Live* belong to their respective rights holders. These assets are unofficial, are not endorsed by those rights holders, and are not covered by the code's MIT license.

The assets are shared for personal, non-commercial use in this DSH companion project. Do not claim ownership of the character or use the assets commercially. If a rights holder requests removal, the artwork should be removed from distributions.
